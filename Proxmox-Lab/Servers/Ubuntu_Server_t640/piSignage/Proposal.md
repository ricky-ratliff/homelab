# Proposal

Proposal to implement Bar & Kitchen digital signage system for Backline Music Hall.

## Explain the problem(s)

Issues with existing traditional menus.

- The lighting makes it difficult for customers to see the menus wile in line, causing a lot of customers to ask what is available at the register and then they feel pressured to order quickly.
  - This can cause customers to avoid going back to the bar as often due to the friction it adds to the transaction for them.
- Some customers aren't aware food is available until they ask.
- On average, there are longer wait times for customers during performance and event breaks when everyone runs to the bar for refills. These rushes may have customers walking away when a performance/event resumes.

Optimizing the transaction process by reducing friction points for customers and staff can reduce the time it takes to serve each customer, allowing for more transactions to occur in the same amount of time.

## Technical Requirements

- Web/Mobile content management system (CMS)
  - Full design & layout control
  - Live updates
  - Scheduled Updates
  - Templates

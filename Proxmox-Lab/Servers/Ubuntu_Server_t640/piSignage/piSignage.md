# piSignage

## Player2

Player2 is a new generation piSignage player based on new Raspberry Pi OS and also available for many more platforms based on Debian/Linux and as PWA/Android apps.

> ⚠️ Important:
Make sure video playback is supported in Chromium browser or VLC with hardware acceleration before choosing your Linux SBC.

## Server

Relevant links for piSignage open source server. Local Server to manage piSignage players based on node.js.

Restaurant Digital Signage - Digital Menu Boards & Promotions

### Dynamic menus and exciting possibilities

Bring your restaurant to life with dynamic menu boards and signage highlighting your stunning food, making it easy for patrons to choose and enjoy an unforgettable experience.

### Why Choose PiSignage

Join thousands of restaurants, cafes, and QSRs using PiSignage to power their digital displays.

### Real-time Menu Updates

Change prices, add specials, or mark items as sold out instantly across all your displays.

### Increase Sales

Dynamic displays can increase sales by up to 30% through strategic upselling and promotions.

### Remote Management

Update your menu boards from anywhere using our web dashboard or mobile app.

### Works Offline

Content is cached locally, so your displays keep running even if internet goes down.

### Scheduling

Schedule your boards to show the right asset at the right time and even turn off when you are not around!

Scheduling in PiSignage makes it easy to choose what shows when on your displays. Set it up once and let it run automatically.

### Plays content even when offline

Once deployed, all your files are stored on the player, so you can keep playing content even when offline.

### Centrally manage your signage solution

Manage all of your locations across the globe allowing for Global, National and local content to play simultaneously.
